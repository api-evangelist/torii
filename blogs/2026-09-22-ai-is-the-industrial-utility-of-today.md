This week, we heard from multiple prospects all looking for one thing: track and control what AI costs, down to tokens, users, and projects, and put everything else on the maybe-later pile. A year ago, those same conversations would have been a mix of offboarding, license use monitoring, and NHI detection. But today, it’s all AI.
