Update — August 11, 2026: This post was published on August 2, hours after Productiv's announcement, and originally advised customers to export their data before the sunset. That is no longer possible. Productiv terminated all customer access on August 5 — a day earlier than the announced August 6 sunset — and states on its website that "all production systems, data stores, and backups have been permanently and securely destroyed.
