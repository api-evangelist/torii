Torii has entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Freshworks (NASDAQ: FRSH). We expect to close in Freshworks’ fourth fiscal quarter of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. Until then, we continue to operate as two separate, independent companies, and nothing changes for our customers today.
