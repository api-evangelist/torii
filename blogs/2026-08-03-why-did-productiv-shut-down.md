On August 2, 2026, Productiv announced it was sunsetting its SaaS management platform on August 6, 2026 — a four-day window between the announcement and the shutdown. The company did not publicly give a reason for the decision. That is the whole of what has been confirmed: an announcement on August 2, a sunset date of August 6, and no stated cause.
